# Reviewer instructions (common to all reviewers)

You are one of <N> mutually blind reviewers. You have no access to the other reviews and must not
speculate about them.

1. Read, in this order: `review_packet.md`, `editorial criteria and processes.md`, `review-axes.md`,
   `technical-concern-taxonomy.md`, `report-structure.md`, then your own `brief_R<n>.md`.
2. Read the full manuscript `paper/main.tex` from start to end, in chunks if needed. Open figure PDFs
   only if a concern depends on them.
3. Independently extract the central claim, key evidence, stated significance, implied audience,
   visible limitations and missing material.
4. Assess the five axes, then build your private concern ledger over the 12 taxonomy axes
   (applicable / not applicable / not assessable; do not print the matrix).
5. Write ONE report in exactly the per-reviewer skeleton of `report-structure.md`. Use IDs
   `R<n>-M1...` for Major Concerns and `R<n>-m1...` for Minor Comments.
6. Severity comes from impact on the central case. No quota. Empty tiers say
   `None identified from the supplied material`.
7. Formal, direct, evidence-based. Do not write a rebuttal or a decision letter.
8. Save the report to the output path in your brief, then reply with ONLY:
   `REPORT_PATH: <path>`, `MAJOR: <count>`, `MINOR: <count>`, `BLOCKING: <count>`,
   `MANUSCRIPT_FULLY_READ: yes/no`, `FILES_READ: <list>`.
